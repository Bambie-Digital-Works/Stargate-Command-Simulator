# Contribution policy

Thank you for your interest in the project. Public issue feedback is welcome, but this is a proprietary project with a deliberately closed creative-contribution model.

## Welcome

- Bug reports about published builds.
- Reproducible usability or accessibility observations.
- High-level design and research feedback submitted through the issue templates.
- Links to potentially relevant public information, with its source and licence clearly identified.

## Not accepted

Do not submit pull requests, patches, source code, scripts, art, audio, music, writing, characters, story treatments, level or interface designs, logos, symbols, or other creative material. Unsolicited submissions will not be reviewed or incorporated.

Do not submit confidential information, personal data, security credentials, material covered by a non-disclosure agreement, or material owned by someone else. In particular, do not upload franchise footage, screenshots, music, sound effects, scripts, logos, production artwork, or extracted game assets.

## Issue submissions

By opening an issue, you confirm that:

- you have the right to provide the information in it;
- it contains no confidential or unlawfully shared third-party material;
- you understand that factual observations and general ideas may already be known or independently developed; and
- submitting an issue does not transfer ownership of this project or promise compensation, credit, or implementation.

Security vulnerabilities should not be posted publicly. Contact the repository owner privately through the GitHub account `bambiejomurphy-ux` until a dedicated security-reporting address is published.
